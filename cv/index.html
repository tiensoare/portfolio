Publications

  • 2026
    Assessing Large Language Models for Stabilizing Numerical Expressions in Scientific Software
    arXiv (in submission to ICSE 2027)

    First evaluation of LLM reasoning for high-precision numerical computation, showing that LLMs fix many expressions that classical tools cannot, making them a complementary tool for numerical robustness.

  • 2025
    Are the Majority of Public Computational Notebooks Pathologically Non-Executable?
    IEEE/ACM MSR 2025

    Large-scale study of 42,546 public Jupyter notebooks showing only 21.3% are truly non-executable (far below prior estimates of up to 76%), plus an LLM-based method for recovering full and partial executability.
