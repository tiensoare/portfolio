**Date:** June 2018 - January 2019

<a href="https://www.facebook.com/events/lincoln-mall/stem-career-day/362215771217724/">Link to event</a>

**Description.** The project is a part of the 2018-2019 Phi Theta Kappa Honors in Action (HIA) program. The main objective of this project is to promote underrepresented communities to join STEM in education and workplaces. It also highly encourages female students and workers to participate in STEM to eliminate the gender gaps. The project was planned, researched, and conducted by the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/centurycollegephithetakappa">Century College Phi Theta Kappa Chapter </a> team, “Alpha Alpha Gamma.”

**Achievement** :trophy:. In the same year at the <a href="https://www.ptk.org/ptk-catalyst-2019-hallmark-awards-gala-recap/">PTK Catalyst 2019</a> (Orlando, FL), our chapter was recognized as one of the best 100 chapter worldwide.
