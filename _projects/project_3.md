**Date:** January 2021 - June 2021

**Team Members:** [Thach Nguyen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thach-nguyen-381b95140/), Tien Nguyen, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/khaled-shouman-ba58311a3/">Khaled Shouman</a>

**Achievement** :trophy: Our team was selected as one of the 6 finalists.

**AbroadstudiZ** was a social innovation project developed as part of the [2021 Fowler Global Social Innovation Challenge](https://business.stthomas.edu/schulze-school/student-engagement/competitions/fowler-global-social-innovation-challenge/) at the University of St. Thomas (Minnesota). The project aimed to make the process of studying abroad in the United States more accessible by bringing essential information and peer experiences together in a single, student-centered platform.

International students often have to navigate a complex series of decisions and unfamiliar processes, from identifying suitable universities and programs to applying for a U.S. visa, securing housing, arranging travel, and adapting to life in a new country. Information is frequently scattered across university websites, government resources, third-party guides, and informal student communities. AbroadstudiZ was designed to provide a centralized starting point that helps students navigate these challenges throughout their study-abroad journey.

The platform organized resources across key stages of the international student experience, including:

- **University and program search:** Resources to help students explore universities and identify programs that match their academic goals.
- **Application guidance:** Information and guidance for navigating the U.S. university application process.
- **Scholarships and financial aid:** Resources for identifying funding opportunities and understanding financial considerations.
- **U.S. visa application:** Guidance and resources for navigating the student visa process.
- **Housing:** Information to help students understand and prepare for finding accommodation in the U.S.
- **Travel and arrival:** Practical guidance for preparing for travel and transitioning into life in the U.S.
- **Banking, phone services, and Social Security:** Resources covering practical administrative tasks that international students encounter after arrival.
- **Cultural adjustment:** Guidance for adapting to a new academic, social, and cultural environment.
- **Student stories:** Firsthand experiences from international students intended to provide practical insights and help students feel connected to others who have gone through a similar journey.

To better understand the challenges faced by prospective and current international students, our team conducted interviews with **three professors and six international students** about their experiences with studying abroad, including the information and support they needed throughout the process. These conversations informed the project's focus on combining practical resources with firsthand student experiences.

**My role in the project was to develop the AbroadstudiZ website**, translating the project's goals and collected resources into a centralized web platform. Through the project, I gained experience in developing a user-facing website while working within a broader social innovation initiative focused on improving the international student experience.
