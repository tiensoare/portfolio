**Description.** The project is a part of the 2019-2020 Phi Theta Kappa Honors in Action (HIA) program. The main objective of this project is to create an environment of connection for individuals looking to improve their mental health through holistic means. The event will begin with a panel covering major aspects of holistic health that includes, mental health, physical health, and nutritional health. In addition, a resource fair will be provided for in-depth information on these topics. The project was planned, researched, and conducted by the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/centurycollegephithetakappa">Century College Phi Theta Kappa Chapter </a> team, “Alpha Alpha Gamma.”
